Statistical Test Selector

An interactive guide to selecting the appropriate statistical test based on your research design, data type, and assumptions.

Use this tool to navigate the complex landscape of statistical testing methods. Answer a few questions about your data and research design, and receive personalized recommendations for the most appropriate test.

About This Tool: The Statistical Test Selector is powered by a comprehensive decision tree based on research methodology best practices. Consult with a statistician for complex designs or when you have questions about assumptions.
