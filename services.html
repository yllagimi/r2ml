Research Capabilities

R2ML combines rigorous epidemiological methods with advanced data engineering to solve complex health challenges. We move beyond simple analytics to provide causal inference and actionable policy insights.

Epidemiology & Research Design

Effective public health interventions require a solid foundation in study design and bias minimization. R2ML specializes in the evaluation of complex health systems and the analysis of longitudinal patient data.

Surveillance System Evaluation

We design and audit surveillance systems according to CDC guidelines to ensure sensitivity, specificity, and timeliness. Our work includes assessing data quality in large-scale registries and identifying gaps in reporting mechanisms for chronic diseases.

Longitudinal Data Analysis

Healthcare data is inherently time-dependent. We utilize advanced statistical methods—including Generalized Estimating Equations (GEE) and Mixed Effects Models—to account for within-subject correlation. This allows us to accurately model disease progression and treatment trajectories over time, rather than relying on static cross-sectional snapshots.

Study Design & Causal Inference

From retrospective cohort studies to quasi-experimental designs, we apply rigorous methodology to distinguish correlation from causation. We employ propensity score matching and instrumental variable analysis to control for confounding in observational real-world data (RWD).

Mental & Behavioral Health

Health behaviors are complex functions of our mental reserves. We apply behavioral economics principles to digital health interventions, creating systems that "nudge" users toward better outcomes when they are most susceptible to decision fatigue.

Just-in-Time Adaptive Interventions (JITAI)

We develop algorithms that detect high-risk contexts (e.g., location, time of day, stress markers) to deliver real-time support. Our "CookieJar" pilot demonstrated how predictive triggers can interrupt binge-eating cycles by offering cognitive reframing techniques at the moment of craving.

Neuropsychological Assessment (ANAM)

We have extensive experience analyzing Automated Neuropsychological Assessment Metrics (ANAM) data. By correlating cognitive performance metrics with medical readiness data, we help defense and health organizations predict cognitive decline and assess fitness-for-duty in high-stress environments.

Data Systems & Interoperability

Analysis is only as good as the data pipeline that feeds it. We engineer robust Interoperability Management Systems (IMS) that connect disparate health records while maintaining semantic consistency.

  • Semantic Standardization: Mapping proprietary schemas to FHIR and HL7 standards.
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP): Extracting structured phenotypes from unstructured clinical notes and pathology reports.
  • Secure API Architecture: Building HIPAA-compliant data exchange layers for multi-hospital research consortiums.

Selected Case Studies & Publications

Predictive Modeling of Healthcare Costs in Chronic Disease Populations

Submitted to the HHS Data Challenge, this project utilized machine learning ensemble methods to identify high-cost outliers within chronic disease patient cohorts. By integrating social determinants of health (SDOH) with claims data, the model achieved a 15% improvement in risk stratification accuracy over traditional actuarial models.

Agimi, Y. et al. "Chronic Disease and Healthcare Cost Predictor." HHS Data Challenge Submission.

H2H Voting Comparator: Visualization of Legislative Data

A Library of Congress Data Challenge submission. We developed a comparator algorithm to visualize voting alignment patterns between legislators. This tool utilized graph theory to map legislative clusters, providing transparency into bipartisan cooperation and polarization trends.

R2 Media Labs. "H2H Comparator C-1000." Library of Congress Legislative Data Challenge.

Digital Phenotyping for Dietary Behavior Change

Investigation into the efficacy of chatbot-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) for dietary adherence. The study utilized the "CookieJar" platform to collect ecological momentary assessments (EMA) of craving intensity and delivered automated micro-interventions.

Internal Pilot Study. R2 Media Labs Behavioral Health Division.
