Our Solutions

Integrated services for surveillance, epidemiology, and predictive public health analytics.

R2ML services are organized to move from data quality and study design to decision support and policy execution.

SurveillanceEpidemiologyRisk PredictionWorkforce

Core Services

Assessments of Public Medical Surveillance Systems

Evaluate surveillance programs against CDC criteria including data quality, completeness, timeliness, and sensitivity to identify reporting and operational gaps.

Epidemiologic Study Design & Analysis

Design and analyze cohort and policy-impact studies using robust causal inference methods and clinically meaningful interpretation.

Population Health Risk Stratification & Prediction

Develop models that identify high-risk groups and support intervention targeting, planning, and resource optimization.

Comorbidity & Disease Pattern Analysis

Map co-occurring condition profiles and trajectories to improve treatment pathway insights and phenotype-aware planning.

Behavioral Health Care Pathway Modeling

Analyze service utilization, treatment adherence, and decision nodes to surface bottlenecks and opportunities for quality improvement.

Healthcare Workforce Analytics & Planning

Assess workforce capacity, deployment, and skills alignment for sustainable staffing and training strategies.

Supporting Capabilities

Data Integration

HIPAA-compliant, cross-source harmonization pipelines for EHR, claims, and registry data.

Clinical NLP

Extraction of meaningful structure from clinical narrative and free-text notes.

Real-Time Dashboards

Executive and operational dashboards for surveillance, care quality, and program metrics.
