About R2ML

Leadership in health data science, epidemiology, and data-driven innovation for public health systems.

Our Mission

R2ML brings academic rigor and data science expertise to solving the most pressing challenges in healthcare delivery, public health policy, and health services research. We work at the intersection of epidemiology, innovation, and health systems to transform data into actionable insights that improve outcomes for vulnerable populations and drive evidence-based policy.

Leadership

Dr. Yll Agimi

Founder & Principal Data Scientist
PhD, MS, MPH

Specializing in Healthcare Delivery and Health Services Research

Dr. Yll Agimi is a Senior Data Scientist specializing in healthcare delivery, health services research, and epidemiology. With extensive experience in both government and private sector, Dr. Agimi has led large-scale research initiatives spanning military health systems, public health policy, and healthcare innovation. Currently serving as a Presidential Innovation Fellow (PIF), he brings a unique blend of academic rigor, policy expertise, and practical implementation experience.

Dr. Agimi has held leadership positions at General Dynamics Information Technology (GDIT), GovCIO, Booz Allen Hamilton, and the Altarum Institute, where he led teams building advanced data solutions for the Military Health System and other major integrated health organizations. His research has examined healthcare utilization patterns, injury epidemiology, and the impact of state and federal policies on population health outcomes.
