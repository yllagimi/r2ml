R2ML Factsheet

A concise snapshot of R2ML’s mission, expertise, and service capabilities aligned with the new suite design language.

Organization

R2ML is a research and analytics organization dedicated to objective analysis and effective solutions for public health and policy decision-makers.

Core Services

  • Surveillance system assessment
  • Epidemiologic study design and analysis
  • Risk stratification and predictive modeling
  • Comorbidity and care pathway analytics
  • Workforce and program planning

Technical Capabilities

  • Machine learning and statistical inference
  • Data integration and standardization
  • Clinical natural language processing
  • Dashboard and decision support development

Markets Served

  • Federal, state, and local health agencies
  • Health systems and provider networks
  • Academic and research institutions
  • Consulting, pharma, and medtech teams
